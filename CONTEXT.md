# Quick Stocktaker

Quick Stocktaker supports counting inventory and moving the resulting stocktake data out of the app for use elsewhere.

## Language

**Stocktake Export**:
A portable file containing the counted data from a stocktake.
_Avoid_: Data export, export file

**Stocktake Delivery**:
The sending, saving, or sharing of a Stocktake Export to a destination outside the app's private storage.
_Avoid_: Upload, data upload

**Stocktake Delivery Workflow**:
The complete process that creates a Stocktake Export and delivers it through a chosen destination.
_Avoid_: Upload workflow, export workflow
